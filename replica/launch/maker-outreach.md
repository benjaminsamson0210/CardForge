# 1-on-1 Maker Outreach & Gift Strategy

The highest-converting cold outreach strategy for design tools is **delivering value before asking for anything**.

Instead of sending generic "check out my tool" messages, find indie hackers and founders who just tweeted a plain, unformatted screenshot of their product, run their screenshot through Shotsmith, and send them the finished studio mockup as a gift.

---

## Strategy: The "Free Visual Upgrade" Workflow

1. **Find Founders:**
   - Search Twitter/X for keywords: `#buildinpublic`, `shipping`, `new release`, `just launched`, `working on my dashboard`.
   - Look for tweets containing raw, rectangular, unpadded screenshots with harsh borders.
2. **Generate the Mockup:**
   - Save their screenshot.
   - Drop it into Shotsmith.
   - Apply the **Midnight Glow** or **Linear Aurora** gradient, turn on the **MacBook Pro** or **macOS Dark** frame, set elevation shadow to `32px`, and export in `2x Retina`.
3. **Send the Direct Message:**

---


*(Attach the exported PNG image directly to the message)*

**Follow-up (Only after they reply "Thank you, this looks great!"):**
```text
Glad you like it! If you ever want to make more, I built Shotsmith (https://shotsmith.app) so founders can generate these in 5 seconds without paying a $12/month subscription.

```
