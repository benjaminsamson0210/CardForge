## 2. Maker's First Comment

Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Benjamin, maker of CardForge.

A few months ago, while auditing our blog's social cards, I realized almost every dynamic Open Graph tool on the market charges $9 to $29 EVERY month. If your card expires or you pause the subscription, every link preview across your entire site suddenly breaks into an ugly empty box on Twitter and Slack.

On top of that, legacy tools that spin up headless browsers often take 2–3 seconds to render, causing Twitterbot to time out and display nothing.

So we built **CardForge**:
- **Sub-50ms Edge Performance:** Powered by Satori and Resvg on Next.js Edge, generating 1200x630 PNG cards instantly with global CDN caching.
- **Parametric URL API (`/m`):** Just pass `?title=...&theme=...`—zero database lock-in or API keys to sync.
- **Studio with Live Scraper:** Paste any URL to auto-extract titles and preview layouts in real-time.
- **Fair Pricing:** A free tier, plus an anti-subscription $49 lifetime deal so your cards never break.

We're offering a launch discount today: use code `HUNTER30` for $39 lifetime access.
