# Brand Guide: CardForge

CardForge is the high-speed dynamic Open Graph and Twitter card generator built for indie founders, technical bloggers, and content creators who want automated social share cards without recurring monthly subscription fatigue.

---

## 1. Name Candidates & Evaluation

| Name | Style | Rationale | Trademark / Web Screening |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CardForge** | Compound (Chosen) | Evokes craftsmanship, strength, and automated card generation. | Clear screening, `cardforge.dev` / `cardforge.io` available. |
| **Metashot** | Compound | Metadata + screenshot. Highly developer-friendly. | Minor collision with meta-game tools. |
| **GraphCraft** | Compound | Open Graph + crafting. | Several legacy graphic tools use GraphCraft. |
| **OgPulse** | Compound | OG tag + vitality. | Somewhat abstract. |
| **SnapPreview** | Descriptive | Quick social preview. | Potential collision with Snapchat trademark families. |

---

## 2. Core Positioning & Pitch
> **For developers, bloggers, and indie makers** who hate paying $108/year just for dynamic Open Graph cards, **CardForge** provides sub-50ms edge-rendered social preview cards with a visual builder, 1-click embed snippets, and a **one-time lifetime option** with zero risk of broken links.

---

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Direct & Technical:** Explain how the edge API works in plain terms (Satori + Resvg, sub-50ms, global CDN caching).
- **Creator-Friendly:** Respect the user's budget and workflow—no forced recurring lock-in.
- **Punchy & Action-Oriented:** "Generate. Embed. Done."

---

## 4. Visual Identity
- **Primary Hue:** Deep Indigo / Electric Violet (`#6366f1` / `#7c3aed`), supported by dark obsidian (`#0f172a`) and crisp white surfaces.
- **Typography:** Inter (open, highly legible on all retina screens).
- **Mark:** Minimal geometric hammer/spark inside a rounded card rectangle (1.9:1 ratio).
