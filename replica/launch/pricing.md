---

## 2. Reviewer Pain Points from Research

1. **Subscription Churn on Micro-utilities:**
   Users cancel after publishing a redesign or finding that $108/year for static blog cards is bad value.
2. **Expired Cards Breaking Live Sites:**
   When a subscriber's card expires, all historical blog posts show broken images on Twitter and LinkedIn.
3. **Aggressive Watermarking:**
   Free users feel cheated when their professional articles display external logos.

---

## 3. The CardForge Pricing Model

1. **Free Tier ($0 Forever):**
   - Unlimited manual web generator usage.
   - Default layout theme.
   - Standard 9 color swatches and patterns.
   - Displays subtle corner CardForge watermark.

2. **Pro Monthly ($9/Month):**
   - Removes CardForge watermark.
   - Unlocks all 4 premium layout themes (Bold, Two-Up, E-Commerce).
   - Custom brand HEX color picker.
   - Cancel anytime with 1 click.

3. **Indie Lifetime Deal ($49 One-Time):**
   - Everything in Pro forever.
   - No monthly bills, zero risk of expired cards breaking historical articles.
   - Ideal for developers, technical writers, and solo founders.
